agblib 1.5.0 adds Engine Software's driver, the one of The Santa Clause 3: The Escape Clause: its detection, a model of it, the game's copy of the driver and its sound manager on the ARM interpreter, and the conversion of its songs to MIDI and SoundFont 2 files. It also adds shared steps for the detections and the conversions of modules. It removes no public API, and it builds on ntdlib 7.0.0 and seqlib 1.5.0.

## New

- `agblib::engine_software` is Engine Software's GBA sound driver: a player of packed modules like FastTracker 2's on a mono software mixer in IWRAM. `engine_software::DetectDriver()` finds the driver by its mixer's code and its routines at their distances from it. It tells the builds of the driver apart and refuses the builds that the model doesn't follow yet, with a message. It reads the bank's address from the game's call that loads it, and takes each song's start from the build's table. `ReadBank()` reads the bank's instruments and songs, and `PitchStep()` gives a note's step. `engine_software::Player` plays a song's rows, ticks, effects and envelopes as the driver does, with the sound manager's ramp of the music volume at a song's start, and mixes its channels into the bytes of FIFO A.
- `engine_software::SetUpSteps()` and `StartSteps()` give the game's set-up and each song's start as steps of the game's code: calls of its routines and stores. `engine_software::SongStart` gives how the game starts a song: a ramp of the music volume, a jingle that plays once, or a song that the game always ends within its first pass.
- `agblib::engine_software_game` runs a game's copy of the driver on `agb::System`: `engine_software::SetUpManager()` makes the sound manager's and the driver's set-up, `engine_software::RunSteps()` runs a list of steps, and `engine_software::GameDriver` calls the manager's start of a song or a jingle and its routines of each frame.
- `agblib::sequence_export_engine_software` converts the driver's songs. `InspectSong()` works out a song's length and loop, `ConvertSong()` writes its MIDI file and SoundFont, and `ConvertSongFiles()` returns both in memory. The game mixes mono. Every note therefore plays at the centre.
- `agb::MatchesThumb()` tells whether a cartridge has given Thumb code at an address, from a list of halfwords with masks or from a pattern's text.
- `agb::kRegDispStat` is the address of DISPSTAT.
- `sequence_export::agb::MissingSongWarning()` and `MissingSongReport()` give the warning and the report of a conversion of a song that the driver doesn't have.
- `sequence_export::agb::Signed8Sample()` makes a SoundFont sample of signed 8-bit points, with its loop lengthened and guarded.
- `agblib/testing/engine_software_cart.h` builds synthetic cartridges with the driver for tests.

## Changes

- The detections of Rare's driver and of MP2K find their Thumb code with `agb::MatchesThumb()`. They find the same drivers.
- `RunModuleConversion()` and the conversions of Webfoot Technologies' driver, Logik State's driver and Frontline Studios' two players take the warning of a missing song from `MissingSongWarning()` and `MissingSongReport()`. The conversions of Frontline Studios' two players make their samples with `Signed8Sample()`. Their output doesn't change.

## Known limitations

- Engine Software's driver: the model starts the music volume's ramp one frame later than the game does. In a song's first 53 frames, the music volume is one step of 4/256 below the game's. Song 5, the jingle, and song 6 start at full volume and aren't affected.
