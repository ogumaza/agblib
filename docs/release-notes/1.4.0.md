agblib 1.4.0 adds Webfoot Technologies' driver, the one of the Dragon Ball Z games of The Legacy of Goku and Buu's Fury, Frontline Studios' MOD player, the one of Hardcore Pool, Garfield: The Search for Pooky and Games Explosion!, and Frontline Studios' XM-like player, the one of Chicken Shoot and Chicken Shoot 2: for each, its detection, a model of it, the game's copy of the driver on the ARM interpreter, and the conversion of its songs to MIDI and SoundFont 2 files. It calls an MP2K song silent when the notes of its chosen tracks all play at a level of 0, and adds `mp2k::Sequencer::SoundedTracks()` for that test. It also sets the music volumes of the Battletoads prototype in Rare's driver, and gives an R&D1 file with a short or overlong header chunk the size that the driver reads. It makes public the sizes of the drivers' records and tables, the layout of the DMA registers and the lines of a trace, and gives a GSF set's start and reserved areas. It removes no public API, and it builds on ntdlib 7.0.0 and seqlib 1.5.0.

## New

- `agblib::webfoot` is Webfoot Technologies' GBA sound driver, a player of packed Impulse Tracker modules on 15 channels of a software mixer. `webfoot::DetectDriver()` finds each program's copy of the driver in a cartridge and the song tables that it plays. It checks the mixer's settings in each program's init against `webfoot::MixerOf()` and gives the result in `SongTable::mixer_check`. A table whose check gives `kDiffers` or `kUnread` has its warning in `mixer_warning`, and the warning's place in `DriverInfo::warnings` in `mixer_warning_index`. For `kSame`, `mixer_warning` is empty and `mixer_warning_index` is 0. `webfoot::FindInit()`, `FindMusicVolume()` and `FindInitRate()` find the driver's init, its setter of the music volume and the rate that the game passes to the init. `ReadSongHeader()`, `ReadSample()`, `ReadIwramImage()` and `OrderCount()` read a song's header, a sample's entry, the image of IWRAM that a program's crt0 copies, and a song's orders. `webfoot::Player` plays a song's rows, ticks and effects as the driver does, and mixes its channels into the bytes of the two FIFOs.
- `agblib::webfoot_game` runs a game's copy of the driver on `agb::System`. `webfoot::FindGameRoutines()` finds the routines that the game calls, and `webfoot::GameDriver` calls them: the init, the setter of the music volume, the start of a song and the driver's interrupt handler. `webfoot::FindMasterHandler()` finds the game's master interrupt handler by its first five instructions, `webfoot::kMasterHandlerCode`. `webfoot::CallHandler()` runs the driver's interrupt handler once as the master handler calls it.
- `agblib::sequence_export_webfoot` converts the driver's songs. `InspectSong()` works out a song's length and loop, `ConvertSong()` writes its MIDI file and SoundFont, and `ConvertSongFiles()` returns both in memory. A ping-pong loop's note plays at the pitch of the voice's step.
- `agblib/sequence_export/agb/module_conversion.h` contains the stages that the conversions of Logik State's modules and Webfoot's songs share: the record of the model's run, the notes of each channel, the timing of the rows, the check of the loop's first pass, the MIDI file and the SoundFont's instruments (`ModuleInstruments`). `LoopPassSamples()` gives the samples of a loop's pass where the mixer checks the loop's end at a fixed spacing and drops the step's fraction at each restart, and `LoopPassesBend()` says whether a note bends to the pitch of those passes.
- `agblib::frontline_mod` is Frontline Studios' MOD player, the older player of the F U H Sound System: a player of modules of 4 channels on a software mixer of 8 channels in IWRAM. `frontline_mod::DetectDriver()` finds the player and the mixer by their code, reads their addresses from their literal pools, and takes each game's set-up and songs from a table of the games. `ReadModule()` and `FindJump()` read a module's header and its jumps. `frontline_mod::Player` plays a module's rows, ticks and effects as the player does, and mixes its channels into the bytes of FIFO A.
- `agblib::frontline_mod_game` runs a game's copy of the player and its mixer on `agb::System`: `frontline_mod::GameDriver` calls the game's set-up, its start of a module at an order and its frames.
- `agblib::sequence_export_frontline_mod` converts the player's songs. `InspectSong()` works out a song's length and loop, `ConvertSong()` writes its MIDI file and SoundFont, and `ConvertSongFiles()` returns both in memory. A note bends to the pitch at which the mixer plays its loop's passes.
- `agblib::frontline_xm` is Frontline Studios' XM-like player, the newer player of the F U H Sound System: a player of modules of 4 channels whose ticks fall inside a frame, on a software mixer in IWRAM. `frontline_xm::DetectDriver()` finds the player by its mix routine's code, reads its routines and tables from their literal pools, and takes each game's set-up and songs from a table of the games: the title's order list, then each distinct list of the game's levels, at the game's master volumes. `ReadModule()` reads a module's header, and `StepOf()` gives a period's step. `frontline_xm::Player` plays a module's rows, ticks and effects as the player does, and mixes its channels into the bytes of FIFO A.
- `agblib::frontline_xm_game` runs a game's copy of the player and its mixer on `agb::System`: `frontline_xm::GameDriver` calls the game's set-up, its calls that start a song and its frames.
- `agblib::sequence_export_frontline_xm` converts the player's songs. `InspectSong()` works out a song's length and loop, `ConvertSong()` writes its MIDI file and SoundFont, and `ConvertSongFiles()` returns both in memory.
- `sequence_export::agb::RunModuleConversion()` runs the steps of a song's conversion that the conversions of modules share, from a driver's run of its model, and `ModuleSongFiles` contains a song's files in memory. The `SongFiles` of Logik State's, Webfoot's and Frontline Studios' conversions are aliases of it.
- `agb::Rom::Version()` returns the software version in the cartridge's header.
- `agb::kRegWaitCnt` is the address of WAITCNT.
- `agblib/testing/webfoot_cart.h`, `agblib/testing/frontline_mod_cart.h` and `agblib/testing/frontline_xm_cart.h` build synthetic cartridges with the drivers for tests.
- `mp2k::JoinOptions::free_floor` gives the earliest place of the free space where `mp2k::Join()` puts the songs that move. In a ROM that contains another driver beside MP2K, the free space then starts past the bytes that the other driver reads, whatever bytes they end with.
- `mp2k_cart::Cart` takes an origin: its driver's parts and data are that many bytes past their usual addresses, and `Cart::At()` gives the address of each. `agblib/testing/two_drivers_cart.h` builds a cartridge with Nintendo R&D2's driver in its first bytes and MP2K after them.
- `mp2k::Sequencer::SoundedTracks()` returns the tracks that played at a level above 0 in some frame. A DirectSound channel counts at a mixer volume above 0 on either side, from the envelope, the channel volumes and, in the SDK's mixer, the master volume. A square or noise channel counts at an envelope level above 0, and the wave channel at a volume that NR32 doesn't mute.
- Seven public headers contain the steps that the drivers share. `agblib/agb/sequence_codec.h` has the parts of a command codec, `agb/cut_steps.h` the steps of a cut and a join, `agb/player_steps.h` the steps of an archive player, `agb/sinc_mix.h` the sinc mix of the enhanced modes, and `agb/sound_registers.h` the names of the sound registers. `agblib/sequence_export/agb/performance_steps.h` has the events of a performance for an editor, and `sequence_export/agb/psg_cycles.h` the PSG samples of the SoundFonts. `agb/system.h` includes `sound_registers.h`, so its register names stay where they were.
- `agblib/sequence_export/agb/module_conversion.h` adds `WriteModuleConductor()`, `FinishModuleSimulation()` and `RunModuleSong()`, the conductor track and the end of a module's conversion.
- `agblib/testing/ubimilan_image.h` adds `WriteBankCalls()`.
- `agb::Rom::GsfEntry()` returns where the CPU of a loaded GSF set starts: the start address of the first program loaded, or 0x08000000 for a cartridge image. `agb::Rom::GsfReserved()` returns the reserved area of each file of the set that has one, in the order of loading.
- `agblib/agb/trace.h` contains the lines of a trace of a driver's model. `agb::PrintHex()` prints bytes in hex, `agb::PrintChanged()` prints a record when it has changed, `agb::TraceRecord` is a record of a frame, and `agb::TraceFrames()` writes a player's records after each frame.
- `agb/system.h` names a DMA channel's registers and the bits of its control: `agb::kDmaStride`, `kDmaCount`, `kDmaControl`, `kDmaEnable`, `kDmaTiming`, `kDmaRepeat` and `kDmaWords`.
- The drivers' headers give the sizes of the records and tables that their layouts read. No value changes.
  - `rd1/instruments.h`: `rd1::RecordBytes()`, the bytes of each kind of instrument record, `rd1::kSampleHeaderBytes`, and `rd1::PointsOfSample()`, the `rd1::SamplePoints` that the driver reads of a sample.
  - `konami/driver.h`: `konami::kSampleHeaderBytes`, `kPackedPairBytes`, the table of pairs of a packed sample, `kSampleNumbers`, and `kInvalidSampleRun`, the run of entries that can't be read that ends a walk of a sample table.
  - `webfoot/driver.h`: `webfoot::kFineSlides`, `kFineSlideBytes`, `kSlideTableBytes` and `kVibratoBytes`.
  - `mp2k/driver.h`: `mp2k::kPlayerEntrySize`, the bytes of an entry of the music player table.
  - `brownie/driver.h`: `brownie::kSampleRecordBytes` and `kSetSamples`, the samples of a set of the Sword of Mana revision.
  - `frontline_xm/driver.h`: `frontline_xm::kWaveSize`, the bytes of each of the player's waves.
- `agb_files::CartImage::SetGameCode()` and `webfoot_cart::TestCart::SetGameCode()` write a test cartridge's game code. `mp2k_cart::Cart::GameCode()` stays as another name of `SetGameCode()`.

## Changes

- An MP2K conversion now sets `SongReport::silent` also when the notes of the chosen tracks all play at a level of 0, such as the notes of a track at a volume of 0. Such a song gets no files, like a song without notes. The MIDI files of the other songs don't change. The other drivers still set `silent` from the notes alone.
- The drivers no longer keep copies of the same code: their codecs, cuts, archive players, mixers, performances and conversions call the shared steps above. Their output doesn't change.
- Konami's detection log line now reads "each sample at its rate".
- The joins of Rare's and Krawall's drivers now throw `std::invalid_argument` for a field whose address would fall outside the GBA's address space, as the joins of R&D1's and AlphaDream's drivers did. Before, they wrote the address wrapped to 32 bits. Only a damaged layout can reach it.
- When a MIDI file can't be written, the conversions of Brownie Brown's and Ubisoft Milan's drivers now give the SoundFont's warnings before the error.
- The headers of ntdlib, seqlib and xsflib now come before agblib's in each source file.
- agblib now builds on ntdlib 7.0.0, xsflib 1.12.0 and seqlib 1.5.0, and asks for ntdlib 7.0 and seqlib 1.5. The detection's runner of Thumb code takes its condition codes, adder and barrel shifter from `ntdlib/arm/alu.h` and `ntdlib/arm/shifter.h`. The conversions name their MIDI tracks with `seqlib::midi::DefaultTrackName()`, and `sequence_export::agb::BendValue()` returns `seqlib::midi::PitchWheelValue()` of the bend over the range. `agblib::rd1_player` links seqlib and writes the MIDI file of a single note with seqlib's writer. The output doesn't change.

## Fixes

- Rare: the Battletoads prototype (header code RARE) now plays tunes 0 and 1 at a music volume of 70 and tune 2 at 80, the volumes that the game sets. They played at the init's 128 before, 5.2 dB and 4.1 dB too loud.
- R&D1: a file with a header chunk shorter than 6 bytes, or one that runs past the bytes, now ends where the driver stops reading: after its last whole track chunk, or after the header's fields if that's later. Before, the file ran to the end of the bytes, and in a ROM to the end of the ROM. `rd1::Cut` then made every later file unusable.
